# Style

Until now, your code has been evaluated solely based on whether the solution is correct. However, correctness is only one aspect of good programming. Readability, maintainability, and simplicity are also important.

In this module, you won’t be solving a new problem. Instead, you will revisit and improve two of your previous assignments: `align.py` and `plot2.py`. Your goal is to refine the style and design of your code, making sure it follows best practices and is easy to understand.

## Style

Ensure your code fully complies with our [style guide](/python/en/style). Pay special attention to:

- [Comments](/python/en/style#comments)  
- [Variable naming](/python/en/style#variables)  
- [Indentation](/python/en/style#indentation)  
- [White spaces](/python/en/style#blank-lines-and-extra-spaces)  
- [Pure functions](/python/en/style#pure-functions)  

## Design

Structure both programs thoughtfully and use functions (in **both** programs) to keep your code organized. The `align.py` assignment already required a `right_align(text)` function, but consider adding more helper functions to further improve the code structure.
